## Overview

Adobe Photoshop Editor is a professional image editing concept built around raster graphics and layered compositions. It brings together AI-assisted generative tools, Camera Raw processing, and Actions automation into a single desktop environment for photographers, designers, and digital artists.

---

## Essential Photoshop Editor Features

- **Layered Editing:** Work with layers, masks, and smart objects in PSD and PSB formats that preserve all editing capabilities .
- **Generative AI Tools:** Use Generative Fill, Generative Expand, and Generate Similar powered by Firefly models, with support for partner models like Flux and Gemini .
- **Camera Raw Filter:** Process raw files and apply non-destructive corrections to TIFF and JPEG images through the Camera Raw filter .
- **Actions Automation:** Record, edit, and play back sequences of commands to automate repetitive edits across single files or batches .
- **Neural Filters:** Access AI-driven filters for portrait adjustments, style transfer, colorization, and super zoom within a dedicated workspace .
- **Cross-Platform Support:** Run on Windows with DirectX 12 GPUs or macOS with Metal support, including Apple Silicon and Windows ARM devices .

## What Adobe Photoshop Editor Brings to Creative Workflows

The editor provides a comprehensive environment for image manipulation and design. Its structure supports both quick retouching and complex multi-layer compositions.

Layered workflows form the foundation. PSD remains the default format that preserves layers, effects, and editing options across compatible Adobe applications, and a composite version can be saved for easier opening elsewhere .

Generative AI extends creative possibilities. The April 2026 release introduced on-canvas object rotation, updated Firefly Fill and Expand models, and a model picker for selecting between Adobe and partner AI models .

Camera Raw integration handles raw processing. Originally designed for digital camera raw files, Camera Raw also processes TIFF and JPEG images, and when used as a filter it remains editable in the Layers panel .

Actions reduce repetitive work. Users can apply ready-made actions, customize them, or record new sequences that resize images, apply effects, or save files in desired formats .

System requirements reflect modern hardware. Photoshop requires a GPU with DirectX 12 support (feature level 12_0) on Windows or Metal support on macOS, with 8 GB RAM minimum and 16 GB recommended .

2. **Obtain Access:** Use the GET button above to reach the designated project destination and review available information.
3. **Complete Setup:** Install Photoshop through Creative Cloud and sign in with your Adobe account.

5. **Create or Open:** Start a new document or open an existing PSD file.
